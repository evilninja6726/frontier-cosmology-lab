# SPECIFIC RESEARCH GAPS & CONTRIBUTION CLASSIFICATION — Milestone 4 & 5

In accordance with Section 6 of our Charter, we do not target vague, famous unsolved problems ("solve dark energy" or "quantize gravity"). Instead, we isolate **specific, mathematically well-posed, computationally tractable gaps** within established physical frameworks and explicitly classify the nature of the potential contribution.
