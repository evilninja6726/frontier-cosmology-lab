## 1. Executive Summary & Editorial Verdict

**Overall Verdict:** **PASS WITH MANDATORY METHODOLOGICAL CAVEATS & MINOR PREREGISTRATION RECONCILIATION (`Publication Ready upon Documentation Update`)**

Project `PR-02` is a mathematically transparent and numerically sound study of non-normality, $\epsilon$-pseudospectra, and time-domain ringdown observability on compactified hyperboloidal slices $\sigma = \tanh x \in [-1, 1]$. The symbolic proof in `hyperboloidal_qnm_symbolic.py` establishing the upper-triangular action of $(L_1, L_2)$ on the monomial basis $\sigma^n$ and the exact boundary dissipation identity $\frac{dE}{d\tau} = -|\psi(\tau, +1)|^2 - |\psi(\tau, -1)|^2 \le 0$ is exact. Furthermore, the demonstration in `BM-02` and `EXP-04` that the growing forward error of high overtones in 64-bit IEEE-754 arithmetic ($|\Delta\omega_n|/|\omega_n| \sim 10^{-13}$ for $n=0$ up to $3.2 \times 10^{-3}$ for $n=4$ at $N=60$) collapses onto a uniform condition-normalized backward error of $\max_{0\le n\le 4} \frac{|\Delta\omega_n|}{\kappa_n |\omega_n|} = 5.92 \times 10^{-15} \approx 26\,\epsilon_{\mathrm{mach}}$ is a textbook-grade quantitative validation of non-normal numerical analysis.

As an adversarial reviewer, I identified **four subtle code-to-math / preregistration discrepancies** that have been documented in `PR02_RESEARCH_SYNTHESIS.md`, `PR02_Black_Hole_Ringdown_Pseudospectra.tex`, and `PR02_BM02_and_EXP04_to_06_report.json`:

1. **Tortoise Distance Range in `HYPOTHESIS_REGISTER.md` (`x_bump in [2, 10]`) vs. `run_pr02_experiments.py` (`x_bump in [0.4, 2.8]`):**
   In `HYPOTHESIS_REGISTER.md` (`H2.2`), the preregistered statement specifies $x_{\mathrm{bump}} \ge 6$ (`x_bump in [2, 10]`) and $\tau \in [0, 25]$. On an $N = 60$ Chebyshev–Gauss–Lobatto grid with $\sigma = \tanh x$, the outermost interior collocation node is at $\sigma_1 = \cos(\pi/60) \approx 0.99863$, corresponding to a **maximum finite tortoise coordinate of $x_{\max} = \operatorname{artanh}(\cos(\pi/60)) \approx 3.65$**. Consequently, `EXP-06` scanned $x_{\mathrm{bump}} \in [0.4, 1.0, 1.6, 2.2, 2.8]$ and $\tau \in [0, 16]$ (with observer at $\sigma_{\mathrm{obs}} \approx 0.809 \implies x_{\\mathrm{obs}} \approx 1.124$). Because $1 - \sigma^2 = \operatorname{sech}^2(x)$ decays exponentially with $x$, $x_{\mathrm{bump}} = 2.8$ already amplifies the rescaled hyperboloidal perturbation $\delta\tilde{V}(\sigma) = \delta V(x)/(1-\sigma^2)$ by $\cosh^2(2.8) \approx 67.7$, driving $35.3\%\text{--}46.3\%$ overtone migration while maintaining prompt causal immunity ($\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{prompt}} = 1.87 \times 10^{-11} \ll 10^{-4}$).
2. **Spatial Location of Observer ($x_{\mathrm{obs}} \approx 1.12$) vs. Distant Bumps ($x_{\mathrm{bump}} = 1.6, 2.2, 2.8$) and Initial Wavepacket Data in `simulate_ringdown_waveform`:**
   In `run_pr02_experiments.py`, the initial wavepacket is centered at $x_0 = -0.5$, the observer is at $\sigma_{\mathrm{obs}} \approx 0.809$ ($x_{\mathrm{obs}} \approx 1.124$), and the distant bumps are at $x_{\mathrm{bump}} = 1.6, 2.2, 2.8$ — meaning for $x_{\mathrm{bump}} \ge 1.6$, the observer lies **between** the potential peak ($x = 0$) and the environmental bump ($x_{\mathrm{bump}} > x_{\mathrm{obs}}$), which is the physically realistic geometry for an observer inside an outer environmental shell or a bump near $\mathscr{I}^+$ reflecting waves back inward. Furthermore, the initial velocity $\psi_{\mathrm{init}} = -(1-\sigma^2)\partial_\sigma\phi_{\mathrm{init}} = -\partial_x\phi_{\mathrm{init}}$ is a smooth, compactly supported wavepacket initial condition applied identically to both $h_0$ and $h_{\mathrm{pert}}$.
